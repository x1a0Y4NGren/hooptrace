HoopTrace is an offline scoring, replay, and player-growth tool for one-on-one basketball. This metadata describes the 2.0.0 candidate; it is not a published release.

Start with temporary names, existing profiles, or a new player. Reuse the last setup, swap sides, review the rules, and choose whether you will mainly keep score or record every shot. The landscape workspace keeps points, misses, fouls, locations, and undo close at hand, with save status, retry feedback, and a skippable guide.

Confirm the final score and recording scope, then open a result summary, full replay, or share report. Link a temporary participant to a profile or start a fresh rematch. Player careers and comparisons show recorded evidence: field-goal and free-throw percentages use separate denominators, and incomplete or zero-attempt samples show no percentage.

Export complete JSON, readable CSV, or a replay image. Replacement and rollback require a verified private safety copy; active matches block replacement. The two latest valid copies are normally retained. Export and import share a 32 MiB, 100,000-rows-per-table, 200,000-total-row capacity contract. Private copies do not replace an external backup.

All records stay on the device by default. There is no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, tracking, or personal-data upload. Optional automatic backup is off by default and writes only to a user-approved folder. The official HoopTrace project remains permanently free and open source under the MIT License.

Simplified Chinese and English, system/light/dark themes, large text, and adaptive layouts are supported. Android is the candidate validation platform; iOS is not formally released. Official signing, same-certificate upgrade, remote CI, and macOS iOS build acceptance remain pending.
