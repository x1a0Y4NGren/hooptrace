HoopTrace is an offline scoring, replay, and player-development tool for one-on-one basketball.

Choose a fast one-tap mode or a detailed court-first mode. Record points, attempts, fouls, possession, match events, and confirmed shot locations. Recover an interrupted active match and use count-up, countdown, or overtime clocks with explicit finish decisions.

Review the game through a local timeline, shot chart, scoring flow, key moments, and analytics that clearly reflect tracking coverage. Profile-linked career views provide 7/30/90-day and all-time growth, opponent filters, recorded shooting trends, and zone heatmaps.

Manage players and scoring rules, archive history, correct finished records with a human-readable audit trail, and export complete JSON backups, CSV data, or a replay image. Versioned restore supports safe replacement and deterministic merge. Optional automatic backup writes only to a user-approved folder with bounded retention.

All records stay on the device by default. HoopTrace has no account, cloud sync, ads, analytics, tracking, or personal-data upload. Automatic backup is optional, disabled by default, and writes only to a folder explicitly selected by the user.

The official HoopTrace project is permanently free and open source under the MIT License.

The complete interface is available in Simplified Chinese and English, with system/light/dark themes, large-text accessibility, and adaptive phone, tablet, foldable, and multi-window layouts. Android is the supported release platform; no iOS binary is promised.
